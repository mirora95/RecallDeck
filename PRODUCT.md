# RecallDeck

An offline study app for self-directed learners on Android and the web. The primary task is creating question/answer cards and returning for short scheduled reviews. No accounts, telemetry or cloud service in the first release.

The current foundation has only an informational landing page. It must say that decks/reviews are unavailable, label future features as planned, and expose no inert controls pretending to be working features. The authoritative behavior and release rules are in the product design under `docs/superpowers/specs`.
