# RecallDeck

An offline study app for self-directed learners on Android and the web. The primary task is creating question/answer cards and returning for short scheduled reviews. No accounts, telemetry or cloud service in the first release.

The shared Home, Decks, deck detail, Review, Progress and Backup routes run in both hosts. Decks lists, creates and renames local decks. Open a deck title to list, create and edit plain-text cards, or explicitly confirm card/deck deletion. Labeled forms show inline validation, loading and empty states, retain input after failed saves and guard submissions. Deck deletion confirms the current card count and atomically removes its cards and review history. Review captures due cards in order, shows each question before an explicit answer reveal, and saves Again or Remembered atomically with its schedule. A failed rating keeps the answer visible and safely retries the same item; missing cards are skipped and the completion summary counts saved reviews and skips. Progress and backups remain planned; expose no inert controls pretending to be working features. The authoritative behavior and release rules are in the product design under `docs/superpowers/specs`.
